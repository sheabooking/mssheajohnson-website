Questions

Good to know.

Should I get the guide or a setup?

Start with the guide if you're comfortable following written steps on your Mac. It's $79 and you go at your own pace. A setup is for when you'd rather have me there with you.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can talk, you can run your assistant. Our first session is about getting you comfortable.

What kind of computer do I need?

A Mac you use for work is the simplest, since that's what I run my own business on. On Windows or a tablet? Ask me, and it's part of your quote.

Will it send emails without asking me?

No. It drafts, and you approve. Nothing goes out until you've read it and said yes.

Is my information safe?

Your assistant lives on your own computer, not on mine. It only connects to the apps you choose, and it asks you before it sends, deletes or buys anything. Your AI plan processes what you ask it, under their privacy rules.

Is there a monthly cost?

Only your AI plan, which you pay to the AI company directly, plus any app upgrades your setup needs. I list those in your quote, so nothing is a surprise.

What's included in a Remote Install?

One session of about 90 minutes over Zoom. I install your assistant with you while you watch, and you type your own passwords. You leave with it working. Ongoing support isn't included.

Where do you travel?

Greater Boston and Massachusetts. If you're farther out, ask me on our call.
