The virtual assistant math
  1. $25 an hour × 10 hours a week = $250 a week
  2. $250 × 52 weeks = $13,000 a year
  3. $13,000 ÷ 12 months = about $1,083 a month
  4. Two years: $13,000 × 2 = $26,000

Questions

Before you reach out

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. If you can talk, you can run your assistant. Our first visit is all about getting comfortable, and I don't leave until you are.

What kind of computer do I need?

A Mac you use for work is the simplest, since that's what I run my own business on. On Windows or a tablet? I can usually make it work, sometimes with a small cloud computer. That's part of your quote.

Will she send emails without asking me?

No. She drafts, and you approve. Nothing goes out until you've read it and said yes.

Is my information safe?

Yes. Your assistant lives on your own computer, not on mine. It only connects to the apps you choose, and it asks you before it sends, deletes or buys anything. Your AI plan (Anthropic or OpenAI) processes what you ask it, under their privacy rules. I also set up a backup drive, so your files always have a second home.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Your assistant checks with you before anything big, so mistakes are easy to catch. Your files are backed up, so nothing is lost. And if you ever want hands-on help, follow-up visits are available.

Is there a monthly cost?

Only your AI plan, which you pay to Anthropic or OpenAI directly, plus any app upgrades your setup needs. I list those in your quote, so nothing is a surprise. Follow-up visits with me are optional.

Can I pay over time?

Yes! Financing is available.

Where do you travel?

Greater Boston and Massachusetts. If you're farther out, ask me on our call.

What if I want more than an assistant?

Once she's running, you can add specialists, like a marketing or strategy agent, priced as part of your quote.
