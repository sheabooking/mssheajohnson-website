I Won’t Tell You What I Found on My Spiritual Journey

My answers aren’t meant to be yours. So I built a path to help you find your own.

Shea Johnson · September 2026 · 1 min read

I created Volume 2 for anyone who might be where I started out on my spiritual journey.

From a small child, I was raised Baptist.

And I understand that spiritual enlightenment is different for everyone.

There were times when my faith was weak…

and that was scary for me.

Especially because I knew I had experienced miracles.

And countless blessings in my life.

So instead of sitting around waiting for answers to come to me…

I went looking.

A journey of self-discovery and spiritual enlightenment.

And I can tell you today, I’m ecstatic about all the things I’ve been learning.

But I won’t share what I’ve learned.

Because what works for me may not work for you.

The point is for you to go on a spiritual journey that works for YOU.

That’s why I made the path universal.

Anyone, from any race, religion, background, region, or walk of life…

can use it to connect with a deeper version of themselves.

What happens inside…

You upload one file to Claude or ChatGPT.

You say hello.

And an AI guide walks with you through five phases, at your pace.

Slowing down long enough to hear yourself again.

Letting go of what you’ve been carrying.

Remembering what you came here to do.

Opening up to abundance.

And building a practice that actually fits your life.

It will never preach, push, or try to change what you believe.

It meets you where you are, and it uses your language.

At the end, it hands you something written only for you…

including one spiritual truth to carry with you.

How to begin…

It’s a guided PDF. No setup needed.

Get Volume 2 on Etsy

Best of luck on your path to spiritual transformation.

And when you’re done, go live what you’ve learned.

With love and light,
