AI Isn’t Scary — But This Is

A letter to busy business owners

Shea Johnson · September 2026 · 1 min read

Dear Business Owner,

You’re “busy.”

I know.

And many of you are leaving money on the table without realizing it.

If you ask me — I don’t think AI itself is scary.

But what is scary?

Being replaced by AI.

Not having a job sooner than you think.

Or not knowing what will happen if your business doesn’t adopt AI in order to stay competitive.

Yet on the flip side…

I know firsthand the wonders of what AI has done for me.

And what it can do for others.

Back in December 2022, I stumbled across ChatGPT several weeks after its release.

I’ve been experimenting with it ever since.

Fast forward to today — and I’ve completed several courses and certifications in business automation and artificial intelligence.

For a long time, I’ve worked with individuals and transformed their lives.

From real estate to credit repair.

From offering simple and practical life advice for immediate implementation — to helping them organize their entire lives.

With certifications like AI for Everyone taught by DeepLearning.ai — I’m a self-taught “course junkie” who loves helping others.

And I can’t wait to help you.

Mo Gawdat made an emergency announcement on the Diary of a CEO podcast.

He stated that all humans must work together to ensure that AI falls into the hands of the good — not those who plan to misuse it or use it for greedy capitalism.

I want to be one of those people on the right side of things.

I’m here to be a leader and teacher.

Helping others leverage AI to increase their revenue while saving time and resources.

Ready to leverage AI for your business?

I’m ready when you are.

Until next time…

Shea 🩷
