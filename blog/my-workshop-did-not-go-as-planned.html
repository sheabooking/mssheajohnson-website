My Workshop Did Not Go as Planned

It went even better.

Shea Johnson · November 2026 · 3 min read

I have such a passion for technology…
and for helping people.

So I decided to bring the two together.

Technology happened by accident for me.

It was one of my personal interests.

Something I kept to myself.

I’ve been on the computer since I was five years old…
and I never got off.

What I’ve learned, and what I’ve taught myself…
I can now share with others.

So they can get back more time with their families and children.

More time freedom.

That was the whole goal of my workshop at Boston Business Mentors (BBM) on headless software.

To help bring the public up to speed with what I’ve been doing for the past nine months…
with my AI agents inside my Apple devices.

I don’t think I’ll ever go back.

I can’t even remember what it feels like to use my laptop…
the way I used to.

I didn’t promote the workshop as much as I wanted to.

There wasn’t much time between the day I scheduled it and the day I went live.

What was most important was that I taught it.

I knew the recording would be available.

And honestly, what matters most is this.

If someone really needs this…
and wants to understand more about what’s happening on the back end…
the workshop is perfect for them.

They can watch it on their own schedule.

It’s on YouTube.

The workshop did not go as planned.

It went even better.

I keep saying it that way because it’s the 100% honest truth.

I was very ill-prepared compared to the way I wanted to do it.

And I still had to show up.

But when I did show up…
I remembered what was important about presenting in the first place.

I also remembered that I wanted this presentation to be different.

I wanted participants to have fun.

I believe learning should be fun.

And you know what?

That’s exactly what happened.

Lots of positive feedback.

Lots of engagement.

I did a great demonstration halfway through.

And at the very end, I let my agents interact with the participants on Zoom…
as a fun closing activity.

When I brought them over to see them talking on the screen…
everyone was blown away.

I had a lot of fun doing this workshop.

AI agents doing work for us.

Using software for us.

Me installing personal AI executive assistants into the pockets of business owners…
so their agents can help them manage their personal and business lives.

Harmoniously.

Seamlessly.

It’s a super powerful integration.

This is by far one of the things I’ve enjoyed most out of everything I’ve done in my lifetime.

Right now, I’m having the time of my life.

I’m loving technology more than ever…
and using it to help people.
