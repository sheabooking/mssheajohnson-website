Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

I'm Shea Johnson, and this is my website. I keep things simple here, and that includes how I handle your information. This page explains what I collect, why, and what I never do with it.

What I collect

  • What you send me. If you fill out a form on this site, I receive what you type, like your name, email, and message. I use it to reply to you and nothing else.
  • Email sign-ups. If you join my email list, your email is stored with SendFox, my email provider. You can unsubscribe from any email at any time.
  • Bookings. If you book a call, Cal.com handles the scheduling and shares your booking details with me.

Those services have their own privacy policies, and they only get what you choose to give them.

What I never do

I don't sell your information, rent it out, or share it with anyone for advertising. Ever.

Shea Agents and Google

"Shea Agents" is my own private tool. It lets my AI assistants save and organize files in my Google Drive, like videos and documents I create for my business. It isn't offered to the public, and the only Google account it connects to is mine.

Shea Agents uses Google Drive data only to move and organize my own files. That data is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else, and never used to train AI models.

Shea Agents' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions or deletion requests

If you'd like to know what I have on file for you, or you'd like it deleted, email me at shea@mssheajohnson.com and I'll take care of it.
