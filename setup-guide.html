Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Every step is copy and paste, explained in plain words first.

How long does setup take?

About an hour and a half, and you can stop and pick up where you left off.

What does Claude cost?

You'll need a paid Claude account. Any paid plan works, and the starter plan is fine. It's paid to Claude, not to me, so check claude.ai for today's price.

Is this only for Mac?

Yes. It works inside your Mac's own apps, like Calendar, Notes and Reminders.

Does my Mac have to stay on?

It needs to be on, but sleep is fine. If your Mac is asleep when a job is due, it runs as soon as the Mac wakes. You can also set your Mac to wake itself before your morning briefing.

Will it send things on its own?

No. It drafts and asks first. Nothing is sent, posted, bought or deleted without your OK.

What about my privacy?

Your assistant lives on your own Mac, and your files stay there. When it works on something, like an email or your calendar, that piece is sent to Claude to process, under your own Claude account's privacy settings. I never see your data.

How do I get my files?

It's an instant download from Etsy. On etsy.com, open Purchases and tap Download Files. The Etsy phone app can't download files, so use a browser or your computer.

What if a step doesn't work?

The guide is self-guided and has its own troubleshooting section. For a download or file problem, message me through Etsy.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final on digital downloads. If a file won't download or open, message me on Etsy and I'll make it right.
