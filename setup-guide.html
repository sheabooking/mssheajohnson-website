Questions

Your questions, answered.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Every command is copy and paste, and the setup guide explains each step in plain words before it does anything.

Is this only for Mac?

Yes, this edition is for Mac. Your assistant works inside your Mac's own apps, like Calendar, Notes and Reminders, and iCloud carries that to your iPhone. On Windows? Ask me about a custom setup.

Is my Claude plan included?

No. Your Claude plan (Pro or higher) is paid directly to Claude. The setup guide and the Executive Assistant skill are what you get from me.

How do I get my files?

Checkout is through my Etsy shop for now, and it's an instant download. After you pay, open Purchases and reviews in your Etsy account and tap Download Files. Use a browser or your computer; files don't download in the Etsy app.

What if I get stuck?

Come back to Claude and paste "Continue my setup." It picks up right where you left off. The guide also has a page of easy fixes for the most common snags.

Can you set it up with me instead?

Yes. A Remote Install is one live session over Zoom where I set it up while you watch. Or I can build a custom setup around your business. See all three ways.
